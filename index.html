01 · 2026.08 — Present · Research Project

Semantic Correspondence and Non-Rigid Registration of 3D Human Shapes

Combined multi-view rendering, DiffMorpher-based image morphing, and cuboid-structured 3D reconstruction to build intermediate guidance point clouds constrained by human geometry for dense semantic correspondence learning through flow estimation. Applied clustering-based downsampling to reduce computation and validated the pipeline on SMPL and SHREC datasets.
